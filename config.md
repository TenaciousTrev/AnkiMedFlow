MedFlow settings. The easiest way to change them is **Tools → MedFlow settings…** (or the **Settings** button under the flowchart editor).

- `arrow_style`: `"straight"` draws arrows as right-angled lines (only horizontal and vertical segments). `"curved"` draws the original curved arrows.
- `card_view`: `"path"` shows, on each review card, only the boxes and arrows leading to the box being asked. `"full"` shows the whole flowchart on every card.
- `hint_bubbles`: `true` shows each box's hint bubble in its top-left corner in the editor (click it to edit the hint). `false` hides the bubbles; hints can still be edited from the ≡ menu in the sidebar.
- `extra_markers`: `true` shows an "extra" marker at the bottom-left of boxes that have an extra (click it to open the extra). `false` hides the markers.
- `toolbar_layout`: where the text-format and zoom bars sit in the editor. `"scroll"` puts them in the top bar, on one row that scrolls sideways when the window is narrow. `"wrap"` puts them in the top bar, which grows extra rows when needed. `"float"` floats them over the canvas as originally designed (format bar top centre, zoom bottom left).
